📚 Cyber Fraud Awareness

Learn about common cyber fraud methods.

🎣 Phishing

Fraudsters may send fake links or messages that look legitimate.

💰 UPI Fraud

Be careful with unexpected payment requests. Never share your UPI PIN.

☎️ Fake Customer Care

Never give OTPs or passwords to unknown people claiming to be support staff.

📈 Investment Scam

Be suspicious of guaranteed-profit offers and pressure to invest quickly.

💼 Job Scam

Be careful if someone demands money before providing a job.

🛒 Shopping Fraud

Check sellers and website addresses before making payments.

🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips

Simple habits can make your online activity safer.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts.

🔢 Never Share OTP

Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords.

🔗 Check Links

Verify website addresses before entering information.

📱 Update Devices

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

🔒 Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever available.

🧠 Think Before Clicking

Don't act quickly because someone creates urgency.
